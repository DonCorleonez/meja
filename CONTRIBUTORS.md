## Attribution and access

These are **project credits** requested by the owner, not assertions that every person or AI service authored a commit in this repository. Individual code contributions, reviews and test evidence should be traceable through commits, PRs and `docs/reviews/`.

GitHub's automatically generated **Contributors** graph is based on Git commit authorship, not this file. The named AI services are not granted GitHub collaborator access by appearing here. Repository access, permissions, release decisions and ownership remain under the human owner's control.

This attribution applies to the MEJA ecosystem; role descriptions do not by themselves imply that every AI service was involved in every repository milestone.
