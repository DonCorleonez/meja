## CA1 — Data, spatial engine and scoring
- Inventory 85 habitat tiles incl 25 keystones, 5 starter clusters, 100 wildlife tokens (20 each bear, elk, salmon, hawk, fox), 21 scoring cards (20 animal A–D plus Family/Intermediate), 25 nature tokens. Define HabitatTile {id, edgeHabitats:[6], allowedSpecies,keystone}, Starter cluster with relative axial cells and orientations, scoring registry by species/card ID. Transcribe exact six edge habitat labels and wildlife icons for each physical tile; validate counts and allowed rotations; do not generate substitutes. 
- Axial hex coordinates q,r, 6-direction neighbor relationships, tile rotation mod6 and connections by matching facing edges for scoring only; placement adjacent at least one occupied hex, no forced matching unless particular official scenario. Separate corridor graph per habitat, each tile contributes at most once to its connected component; wildlife neighbor graph independent of tile terrain. State has market 4 independent tile/token slots, stacks/bag, transient picked items, nature pool, player maps and rounds, selected scoring set, solo discard order; pure legal action/reducer with explicit overpopulation, nature spend, selection, tile placement, wildlife place or return, replenish, next and scoring phases. 
- Match setup exact tiles per player count incl solo, random starters/scoring set and initial market. Mandatory four-of-kind wildlife refresh repeat per rule, optional three-of-kind once per turn, with removed tokens withheld until replacement then returned to bag; use Nature Token to decouple any tile/token or wipe chosen wildlife subset, repeat only as rules permit. Place tile and token in either legal order; allow token on any available matching tile, choose return if no valid/declined. Reward keystone placement with Nature Token; reconcile finite supply handling. Refill vacancies and maintain market alignment; equal 20 turns per player. Solo official display discard/slide/refill orientation and exact 20 turns, no fake bot opponent in official solo.
- Scoring engine pure per wildlife card A–D (20 separately tested functions): bears grouping/adjacency, elk formations, salmon paths and run limits, hawk isolation/line of sight, fox neighboring diversity/pairs; derive exact conditions/point charts from official card/rule examples, not approximate heuristics. Compute scoring evidence objects listing locations/groups and points; overlapping/double count requirements per card. Deterministic component/graph traversal; complex grouping/optimization if card requires maximum disjoint groups. Score largest habitat corridor per each five habitat types, size + published 2/3/4-player majority/second-place ties; solo corridor ≥7 bonus, unused nature points; exact tie rule. Include Family/Intermediate card scoring and 15 solo scenarios with verified constraints/achievement thresholds; official multiplayer achievement restrictions separate selectable modes with distinct rule IDs (if not supported label explicitly, no claim). 
- Tests: tile schema and rotated edges, disconnected matching habitat, multi-habitat tiles, graph component/corridor and adjacency, every animal card official example plus counterexamples/overlaps, hawk line/occlusion, salmon branch, tie bonuses, solo discard edge incl split pick, nature token and mandatory/optional refresh, token conservation incl returns/discards, placement legality after rotation, 20 turns, scenario and score golden fixtures. ≥1k simulations per aggregate modes, score invariants, deterministic replay.

Phone market bottom tray, map central, scoring drawer; tablet split map and market; desktop full tabletop. No huge asset libraries, no copied official art; original icons and layouts preserving game function.
