## F71 — Deck and rules
- Data schema discriminated Number, Modifier, Action; verify deck against physical/official inventory: 79 numbers (one 0 and n copies of 1–12), 6 modifiers (+2,+4,+6,+8,+10,x2), 9 action (3 each Freeze, Flip Three, Second Chance), 94 total. Unique IDs and source. 
- Model deck/discard/table cards and player state (active/stayed/frozen/bust), global round/total score, dealer rotation, cursor, pending forced draws/actions, phase; setup.ts/turn.ts/actionCards.ts/reducer.ts/legal.ts/scoring.ts/view.ts. Dealer deals initial cards with exact action resolution timing, normal Hit/Stay per active player; no future draws visible. Distinct numbers only, 0 counts for uniqueness but not score, duplicate busts unless Second Chance applies. Resolve action target among legally active players incl self; when last active, self-target mandatory. Freeze locks current score, Flip Three forces draws subject to bust/Flip7 and exact queued-action precedence, nested action targeting, Second Chance duplicate protection and extra Second Chance disposition per FAQ. Verify whether action cards during forced draws resolve immediately or queued and how round ends; no arbitrary simplification. Discard/reshuffle exactly per rules (not full deck reset if cards still on table); correctly end round if no active player or Flip 7. 
- Score numbers sum, x2 only multiplies numbers, then plus modifiers, +15 for seven distinct numbers; bust zero; thresholds and end round totals, 200+ victory, tie additional rounds per official FAQ. Verify edge exact 200 tie and scoring of 0/negative in variants; exclude house rules. End-of-round ledger all card categories and totals; card conservation across deck/discards/held/table/resolving. 
- Tests: inventory, initial deals/action chains, dealer rotation and skipped seats; repeated 0, duplicate after protection, multiple second chance, forced Flip Three bust mid-chain and Flip7 early stop, nested targeting, freeze self/others, last player, no active player, modifier order, deck exhaustion, reshuffle, exact 200/over ties, 3–18 seats, replay + hidden info. Reconcile rulebook/FAQ on edge cases, not invented behavior.
## F72 — Original card-table design
Bold retro neon gaming-table aesthetic with high contrast and prominent numerals, original card fronts/back (no original logos/art), central dealer deck/discard, player lanes with unique number count, modifiers and action markers. Sticky Hit/Stay; clearly show Bust/Frozen/Banked, live round scoreboard and final rankings; target selection sheet, multi-draw progress, second chance shield and Flip7 celebration. Compact grid and scrollable opponent lanes for large tables, no core action hidden. View all public cards; optional teaching risk display derived only from public history and known inventory, off by default in competitive mode if it changes physical experience. 
## F73 — Immersion
Physical-feel flip animation, card dealing/stack/bust/glow, distinct optional sound, speed control, no hidden next card leak. Tutorial Hit/Stay and actions, score explanation and round history, rematch. Offline autosave at pending choices/forced draws; deterministic skip/replay.
## F74 — AI
Easy risk preference; Normal expected value of next draw vs banking based on public known remaining composition without true deck order, protection and modifiers; Hard considers remaining game threshold/leader, action target and multi-draw risk using sampled unknowns. Use multiple bot personalities (cautious/balanced/aggressive) independent of difficulty. Seeded benchmark 200+ per matchup per seat category, average score, bust rate, Flip7 rate, win rates and latency; no privileged deck/seed. 
## F75 — RC
One-deck official mode 3–18, solo human with at least 2 AI and mixed seats, all action interactions and 200 ties, full browser/offline QA and review. Larger deck/alternate variant explicitly outside initial scope.
